# Naïve Autonomous Company Hackathon (YC, SF)

Source: https://events.ycombinator.com/naive-autonomous-companies-hackathon (read 2026-11-06).

## The ask

"Build an agent-run company that runs itself." Choose a business, build its team with
the **Naïve SDK** (required for all teams, docs: usenaive.ai/docs), and put it to work:
finding customers, delivering the product, and running day-to-day operations.

At demo time, show the company **operating**: not what it could do, but what its agents
actually accomplished. What did they deliver? What decisions did they make? Where did
you need to step in?

**Judging focus: how much of the company runs without you.** Live demos.

Example areas (not required tracks): media (agents pick, produce and iterate content
on audience results), agencies (prospect, scope, deliver, follow up), commerce (pick
products, run campaigns, handle customers, adjust on sales), software (build, acquire
users, support, improve from feedback).
