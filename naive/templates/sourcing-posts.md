# Client-sourcing posts

Marketing posts the X and LinkedIn ones with `utax.post_social` (channels `x`, `linkedin`, `kind:
"client"`). The Discord/Slack text is for the owner to post by hand (fill in the two markers):
`post_social` has no such channel.

Rules: friendly, one call to action ("talk to U-Tax"; the gateway appends the firm's Talk to U-Tax
link), "educational estimate, not tax advice". The only dollar figures allowed are the firm's standard
example: $85,165, $400, $450, $50 (the gateway refuses any other). Never a client's name, case or
result, and no links, @handles or email addresses: the guard refuses them. X posts stay under 255
characters so the appended link still fits in 280.
